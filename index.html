MSc Final Thesis · 2026

Behavioural and Lifestyle Factors Associated with Mental Wellbeing Among International Students in Birmingham: A Quantitative Cross-Sectional Study

This study examined associations between sleep disturbance, perceived stress, dietary behaviour and mental wellbeing among international students in Birmingham using validated measures and hierarchical multiple linear regression.

120participants
40.9%variance explained in final model
β = −0.452sleep disturbance, strongest predictor

Student wellbeingSleep · Stress · Diet · Context

Key finding: greater sleep disturbance and higher perceived stress were independently associated with lower mental wellbeing. Dietary behaviour was not independently significant after adjustment.
